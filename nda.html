THIS NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of the date of electronic acceptance (the "Effective Date") by and between Shrim LLC, a Florida limited liability company, located at 1585 Breakwater Terrace, Hollywood, FL 33019 ("Shrim LLC"), and the individual accepting this Agreement electronically ("Recipient"). As used herein, Shrim LLC is the "Disclosing Party" and the accepting individual is the "Recipient."
1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a business opportunity of mutual interest — specifically, the early-access testing of Curiosity Detective, an AI-powered educational game concept (the "Opportunity"). In connection with the Opportunity, Shrim LLC may disclose to the Recipient certain confidential technical, creative, and business information that Shrim LLC desires the Recipient to treat as confidential.

2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by Shrim LLC to the Recipient, either directly or indirectly, in writing, orally, or by inspection of tangible or digital objects, including without limitation: the game concept and design, system prompts, Soul Files, educational frameworks, business plans, product plans, research, processes, specifications, software, source code, or any other information that Shrim LLC identifies as confidential or proprietary. Confidential Information shall not include information which (i) is or becomes publicly known through no act or failure of the Recipient; (ii) is in the Recipient's possession prior to disclosure; (iii) is obtained by the Recipient from a third party without breach of confidentiality obligations; or (iv) is independently developed by the Recipient without reference to Shrim LLC's Confidential Information.

3. Permitted Use

The Recipient agrees to use Shrim LLC's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of participating in the early-access testing of Curiosity Detective. The Recipient shall not reverse engineer, reproduce, distribute, or commercially exploit any materials shared. The Recipient shall not make copies of Shrim LLC's Confidential Information without prior written approval.

4. Maintenance of Confidentiality

The Recipient shall maintain the confidentiality of Shrim LLC's Confidential Information using at least commercially reasonable measures. The Recipient agrees not to disclose any Confidential Information to third parties. This includes but is not limited to: sharing the system prompts, describing the game mechanics in detail, posting about the concept on social media, or otherwise disclosing the nature of the Opportunity to individuals outside the designated test group.

5. Disclosure Required by Law

In the event the Recipient is required by law or valid court order to disclose any of Shrim LLC's Confidential Information, the Recipient shall promptly notify Shrim LLC in writing so that Shrim LLC may seek a protective order or other appropriate relief. The Recipient shall cooperate with Shrim LLC in seeking such relief and shall furnish only the portion of Confidential Information legally required.

6. Immunity Notice

Pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b), the Recipient will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any Federal or State trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a Federal, State, or local government official, or to an attorney, for the sole purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law. Nothing in this Agreement is intended to conflict with 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b).

7. No Obligation

Nothing herein requires Shrim LLC to disclose any particular Confidential Information, to proceed with the Opportunity, or to enter into any further agreement with the Recipient. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating any joint venture, partnership, or other formal business relationship between the parties.
