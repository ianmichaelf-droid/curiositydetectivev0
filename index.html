Curiosity
Detective

A 20-minute game where your kid learns critical thinking by catching AI in a lie.

The Game

It's played with any AI assistant — Claude, ChatGPT, or similar. The parent pastes a system prompt that turns the AI into the Curiosity Detective: a warm, engaging character who explores any subject with the child, but deliberately slips in plausible-sounding errors.

The child's job is to catch them. When they do — or when they don't — the AI reveals what it made up, and the two of them audit the session together. Every session ends with a report for the parent.

The game is built around the correction, not the error. Either way, the session ends with the child understanding that confident delivery and accurate content are different things.

Setup · 2 Minutes
1
Open Claude.ai or ChatGPT on your phone or laptop and start a new conversation.
2
Choose the age group below, tap the copy button, and paste it as your first message. Hit send.
3
Tap the voice/speaker icon and say "hi" to wake the AI up. It should respond out loud.
4
Put the device down — or hand it to your child face-down. This game is designed to be screen-free.
5
Let your kid lead. You just need to be present. Answer if the AI addresses you directly — otherwise let it run.
Nothing to install. No account to create beyond the AI assistant you already have.
Choose a Prompt · Copy & Paste

Pick the tier that matches your child's age. Each version is calibrated for a different level of reasoning. Tap to copy the full prompt — then paste it into any AI.

Ages 6–8
Curious Cub
Soul File v1.5

Warm, playful, child-led. The AI slips in confident-sounding errors and helps the child catch them. Core question: "Is that true?"

Ages 8–10
Field Detective
Soul File v1.8

Introduces slip patterns: Wrong Fact, Big Stretch, Missing Piece. Builds verification as a reflex. Core question: "How would we find out?"

Ages 10–12
Senior Investigator
Soul File v2.0

Source laundering, statistical sleight of hand, false balance. For kids entering social media. Core question: "Where did this come from, and who benefits?"

What Happens in a Session
🕵️
The child picks a topic. Anything — animals, space, sport, video games, history. The AI follows their lead entirely.
🎭
The AI slips in errors. Plausible, confident-sounding, age-appropriate. Never harmful. The child tries to catch them.
💡
The reveal happens. Whether the child caught the slip or not, the AI walks back through what it said and what was real. No gotcha — collaborative.
📋
The parent gets a report. What was said, what the child caught, a question for the parent, and a note for the next session.
❓
The session ends with a question. "Did anything we talked about change how you were thinking at the start?" Every time.

This is an early access test. Three sessions have been run with one child. We want to know if it works with yours. If you play a session — even one — share what happened. What did your kid do? What surprised you? That's the feedback that matters.
